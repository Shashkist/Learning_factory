# Copy and move

There are two files in `~/inbox`: `report.txt` and `photo.txt`. The folders `~/backup` and `~/pictures` are waiting.

1. Put a **copy** of `report.txt` in `~/backup`. The original stays in `~/inbox`.
2. **Move** `photo.txt` to `~/pictures`. It must not stay in `~/inbox`.

Hint: one of the commands `cp` and `mv` leaves the original in place, the other does not. Use `ls` to check both folders.
