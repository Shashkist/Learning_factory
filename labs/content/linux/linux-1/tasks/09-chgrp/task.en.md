# Owner and group

Every file has an owner and a group. Run `ls -l ~/team-notes.txt`: the third column is the owner, the fourth is the group.

Right now the group is `student`. Change it to `team`, a group you belong to. You can see your groups with `groups`.

Hint: `chgrp` changes the group of a file. You may only give your files to groups you are a member of. (Changing the owner is different, see the next quiz.)
