# Ask the service

A small service is running on your machine at `127.0.0.1`, port `9091`. When you ask it for its page, it answers with one line.

Ask it with `curl` and save the answer in the file `pong.txt` in your home folder, exactly as the service sent it.

Hint: `curl` prints the answer of the address you give it. Send the output into the file with `>`.
